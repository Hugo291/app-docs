Nonogrammes — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026 · Version 1.11

No data collection on our side

Nonogrammes does not collect any personal data and has no server. The app is a calm logic game (picross / hanjie / nonograms) that requires no account and never identifies the user. Your preferences and your progress (solved puzzles, statistics) are stored exclusively on your device, in the app's local database. Only the two Google services described below, the optional rewarded video (Google AdMob) and the Full version purchase (Google Play), may exchange data with Google. No game data (puzzles, progress, preferences) is ever sent to them.

No personal data sent to our servers

The app does not transmit any data to our servers (we have none). No account, no cloud, no synchronization. Puzzles are stored in the app and the game works 100% offline; only a purchase and the rewarded video need a network connection.

No account required

You do not need to create an account to use Nonogrammes. We do not ask for an email, name, phone number, or any other identifying information.

Optional rewarded video (Google AdMob)

Nonogrammes is free and contains no banners, no interstitials and no forced advertising. The only advertising is a rewarded video that never starts without a tap from you: it unlocks 10 more drawings beyond the 50 free ones, a theme, or an extra hint. You never have to watch one: the first 50 drawings, the daily puzzle and each puzzle's hints stay free, and the Full version (see below) unlocks everything without videos.

Consent (GDPR). Where the law requires it (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland), Google's consent message (UMP) is shown before your first video. You can accept or refuse personalized advertising there, and change your choice at any time from the app's Options screen, row “Privacy options (ads)”.

This video is provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). When you start it, Google may process, to show the ad, measure its performance and prevent fraud: your IP address (approximate location), device identifiers including the advertising ID, your interactions with the ad and diagnostic data. This data is encrypted in transit and processed by Google under its own policy (policies.google.com/privacy). We have no access to it. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings (Google > Ads).

No other analytics or behavior measurement tool (Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.) is included.

Purchase via Google Play

The app offers a single, optional, one-time purchase: the Full version, which unlocks every drawing and every theme and removes the videos. No subscription. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play: we receive no payment data, only the purchase confirmation, kept on your device to unlock the content.

Local data storage

Your preferences and progress are stored locally on your device by the app (built with Flutter, local Hive storage, for Android). This data never leaves your phone. The daily reminder, if you turn it on, is a notification scheduled locally by your phone, with no network.

Permissions and sharing

The app uses share_plus, which opens your operating system's native share sheet when you explicitly initiate sharing a screenshot or text (for example, a solved puzzle). No sharing happens without your action, and the app has no access to any of the destinations you choose to share with.

Deleting your data

You stay in full control of your data. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all the data it has stored on your device.

Children

Nonogrammes does not collect any personal data, from children or adults, and asks for no identifying information (name, email, age, location). The only advertising is the rewarded video described above, never shown without a deliberate action from the user. Parents or guardians can refuse personalized advertising (consent) or never start a video: the game stays playable.

If you have a question about a child's privacy, please contact us at the address below.
