Triple 20 — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026 · Version 1.1 · com.hfc.flechette

This policy applies to the Android app Triple 20 ("Triple 20 – Darts Scoreboard" on Google Play, com.hfc.flechette), published on Google Play by PIXELMORE (Hugo Ferreira).

No account, no data collected by us

PIXELMORE collects no personal data through Triple 20. The app asks for no account, no sign-up, no email address and no phone number. It does not identify you, and we receive nothing from your device. Only one third party is involved: Google, for the ads described below.

Your game data stays with you

The player names you save, your games, your statistics and your settings are stored only in your phone's private storage. They are sent neither to us, nor to Google's ad network, nor to any server of the app, which has none. The one exception is up to you: if Android backup is turned on on your phone, Android includes them, as it does for any app's data, in your Google account backup, which we have no access to.

Network access: for ads only

Triple 20 works entirely offline. Since version 1.1.0, it connects to the internet for advertising, and for nothing else: at each launch, to ask Google whether consent is required and then start the ad service; at the start of a game, to prepare the ad shown when it ends. Without a connection, in airplane mode for example, no ad is shown and the app behaves exactly the same.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Triple 20 shows one full-screen ad (interstitial) at a single moment: when you leave the results screen of a finished game ("Rematch", "Back to home", or the back button or gesture). Never during a game, never when the app opens or closes, and at most one every three minutes. The ad service starts when the app launches, after the consent form where one is required; the ad is requested at the start of the game so that it is ready at the end. If it is not ready, nothing is shown and you carry on without waiting. Ads are limited to the "everyone" content rating.

These ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To show and measure them and to prevent fraud, Google processes: the Android advertising ID and other device identifiers, your IP address (and therefore an approximate location), device information (model, Android version, language), your interactions with the ads (ad shown, tapped, closed) and diagnostic information (launch time, hang rate and energy use of the ad module). This data may be processed on Google servers located outside the European Union. Google then acts as an independent controller, under its own rules: how Google uses information from partner apps and Google Privacy Policy. We receive none of this data: AdMob only shows us totals (number of ads shown, revenue).

Your consent

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown when the app launches, before any ad is requested. You can accept, refuse ("Do not consent") or choose purpose by purpose ("Manage options").

  • If you accept, Google may use the advertising ID and store information on your device to personalise and measure ads.
  • If you refuse, the app works exactly the same; Google then only serves non-personalised or limited ads, chosen without your profile.

You can change your mind at any time, as easily as you gave your answer: Settings → "Ad privacy" reopens the same form. Your new answer applies at once: the ad already prepared is discarded, and the next one is only requested under your new choices. This entry appears where the regulations require it. Everywhere, you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Privacy → Ads; the exact wording varies by phone).

No other trackers, no analytics

Apart from the advertising described above, there is no analytics tool (no Google Analytics, no Firebase), no crash reporting and no other third-party tracking library. No data is sold, rented or passed on to data brokers.

Android permissions

The app uses no sensitive permission: no camera, no microphone, no location, no contacts, no storage, no notifications. The permissions it declares all serve advertising, apart from one internal permission:

  • Internet (INTERNET) and network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) — loading and showing ads. No game data goes through this connection.
  • Advertising ID and Android advertising services (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS) — added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, within the limits of your consent.
  • Background tasks (WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE) — added by the Android WorkManager library, which the ad SDK uses.
  • Internal permission (com.hfc.flechette.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION) — declared automatically by the Android AndroidX library and specific to the app: it keeps the app's internal messages private and grants access to nothing on your phone.

The app keeps the screen awake during a game if you turn that on in the settings, and uses the system text-to-speech engine to call the scores if you turn that on too. Both stay on the device.

Deleting your data

The settings offer "Reset statistics", which clears the history and the averages. Uninstalling the app, or using Android Settings → Apps → Triple 20 → Storage → Clear data, permanently removes the game data from the phone. If Android backup is turned on, a copy may remain in your Google account backup and come back when you reinstall; you manage it in your phone's backup settings. The data Google processes for advertising is not in our hands: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, and address your requests to Google.

Your rights

We hold no data that could identify you: your game data stays with you, and the advertising data is held by Google, tied to an advertising ID, never to your name. Under the GDPR, you have the right of access, rectification, erasure and objection. Where the law requires it (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland), advertising rests on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time from Settings → "Ad privacy". Requests about the data Google processes for advertising should be addressed to Google; for any other question, write to us at the address below. You may also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority (CNIL) or with the one in your country.

Children

Triple 20 is not directed at children under 13: its declared audience on Google Play starts at 13. The content itself is rated for everyone (PEGI 3), and ads are limited to the "everyone" content rating. The app has no account, no in-app purchase and no exchange between players.

We collect no data at all, and therefore none from a child; only advertising goes through Google, under its own rules. If you are a parent or legal guardian and have a question, write to us at the address below.
