Triple 20 — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026 · Version 1.0 · com.hfc.flechette

No data is collected

Triple 20 collects no personal data. The app asks for no account, no sign-up, no email address and no phone number. It does not identify you.

No data leaves your device

The player names you save, your games, your statistics and your settings are stored only in your phone's private storage. Nothing is sent over the internet, to us or to anyone else. The app has no server.

No network access

Triple 20 works entirely offline and does not request internet permission. You can check it yourself: the app behaves identically in flight mode.

No ads, no trackers, no analytics

There is no ad network, no analytics tool, no advertising identifier, no cookie and no third-party tracking library.

Android permissions

The app uses no sensitive permission. It keeps the screen awake during a game if you turn that on in the settings, and uses the system text-to-speech engine to call the scores if you turn that on too. Both stay on the device.

Deleting your data

The settings offer "Reset statistics", which clears the history and the averages. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all data, since no copy exists anywhere else.

Children

As the app collects no data at all, it collects none about children.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page, with a new update date.
