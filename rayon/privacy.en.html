Rayon — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 August 2026 · Version 1.0

No data collection

Rayon does not collect any personal data. The app is an optical puzzle game that runs entirely offline, with no account and no sign-up. Your progress (completed levels, stars earned, remaining hints) and your settings (sounds, music, vibration, reduced motion, patterned targets, language) are stored exclusively on your device.

No data sent to any server

The app transmits no data, neither to us nor to third parties. There is no account, no cloud, no synchronisation. Levels are bundled inside the app: no network connection is required to play.

No ads, no trackers

Rayon shows no advertising. The app contains no analytics, no measurement tool, no crash reporter, and never reads your device's advertising identifier. No sensitive Android permission is requested.

In-app purchases

Rayon offers two optional purchases: a hint pack and a full version. No purchase is required to finish the game. These purchases are handled by Google Play, not by the app: Rayon never sees, receives or stores any payment data. Payment processing by Google is covered by Google's privacy policy. The app only records locally that a purchase was made, so it can grant you its benefit.

Children

Rayon is not specifically directed at children, but its content is suitable for all audiences. Since no data is collected, no children's data is collected either.

Your rights

As no personal data is collected or held outside your device, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. You retain full control of the local data: it is erased from the app's Android settings, or by uninstalling it.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published at this address, with a new update date. Should a future version of Rayon collect data, this page would be updated before that version is released.
