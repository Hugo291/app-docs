Yam's — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026 · Version 2.0

This privacy policy applies to the Android application “Yam's: Offline Yatzy Dice Game” (com.hfc.yams), published on Google Play by PIXELMORE (Hugo Ferreira).

Application: Yam's: Offline Yatzy Dice Game  ·  Publisher: PIXELMORE (Hugo Ferreira)  ·  Contact: hugoferreirapro803@gmail.com

Data collected

Yam's does not collect any personal data for its own purposes. The app is a dice game (yatzy) that requires no account and never identifies the user. Your settings and your progress are stored exclusively on your device. One exception: the advertising described below, whose data is collected by Google, not by us.

No data sent to our servers

We have no server: no account, no cloud, no synchronization of your information. The game works offline; the connection is only used by two Google services: Google Play, if you make the one-time purchase, and Google AdMob, to show the ads described below. Your games, player names and scores are never transmitted.

No account required

You do not need to create an account to use Yam's. We do not ask for an email, name, phone number, or any other identifying information.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows very little advertising: one full-screen ad when you leave the end-of-game screen, never during a turn. No ads during the first 3 days of use, then at most one ad every 3 finished games and 2 per day. The one-time purchase removes all ads: once purchased, the app loads none.

Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). This SDK collects and shares with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention: the IP address (which can be used to estimate an approximate location), interactions with the app (launches, taps, videos viewed), diagnostic information (launch time, crashes, power use) and device identifiers (the Android advertising ID, App Set ID). This data is encrypted in transit. We have no access to it and use no other analytics or tracking tool (no Firebase, no Google Analytics).

Where the law requires it (European Economic Area, United Kingdom…), a Google consent form is shown before any ad; you can reopen it at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options. You can also reset or delete the advertising ID in Android's settings. Details: Google's advertising policies.

One-time purchase via Google Play

The app offers a one-time purchase (no subscription) to unlock the full version. This purchase is handled entirely by Google Play (Google Play Billing): we never see, receive or store any payment data — no card number, no identity, no billing address. The only effect inside the app is a "purchased" flag stored locally on your device, which also lets you restore your purchase after a reinstall. Google's processing of the payment is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Permissions

The app requests no sensitive Android permission: no location, no contacts, no photos, no microphone, no camera, no shared storage. The permissions declared are added by Google's modules: com.android.vending.BILLING (one-time purchase), INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (talking to Google Play and AdMob), com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID and the ACCESS_ADSERVICES_* permissions (advertising ID and Android's advertising services), plus a few technical permissions of the advertising SDK.

Local data storage

Your settings and your progress are stored locally by the app (built with Flutter) using Hive, a local database kept in a private file that the system reserves for the app and that no other app can access. Specifically, it holds:

  • The name you type — asked once on first launch so it can appear on the score sheet, editable or erasable in the settings. It stays on the device and is never transmitted.
  • Your settings — light/dark/system theme, dice animation, bonus or bonus-free variant, player count and locally typed player names.
  • Your statistics — games played, best game, average score, number of Yam's rolled, personal record.
  • The game in progress — score sheet and dice, so you can resume after closing the app.
  • The purchase flag — a simple "purchased" marker once multiplayer, the Easy and Ruthless levels and statistics have been unlocked.

This data never leaves your device.

Deleting your data

You remain in full control of your data. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes everything it stored on your device. On Android you can also use Settings → Apps → Yam's → Storage → Clear data. Your one-time purchase stays attached to your Google Play account and can be restored after a reinstall.

Children

Yam's is aimed at an adult audience and does not specifically target children. The game has no player-to-player communication and asks for no personal data, whatever the age of the person playing; ads are not aimed at children.

Your rights

We hold no data about you: there is nothing on our side to access, correct or delete. The only data that exists is the data that stays on your device, and it is yours to manage. For payment- or advertising-related data, please contact Google (Google Play, Google AdMob), the only party that processes it: see Google's privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app changes significantly. If a future version were to add anything that collects data, this page would be updated before that version goes live. The last-updated date is shown at the top of this page.
