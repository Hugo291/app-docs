Anagramme chrono — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026 · Version 1.0

No data collection

Anagramme chrono does not collect any personal data. The app is a word game that runs entirely offline, with no account and no sign-up. Your nickname (chosen by you, never verified nor transmitted), your progress (remaining hints, league points and tier, your duel history) and your settings (sounds, vibration, colour-blind mode, timer effects, language) are stored exclusively on your device, in the app's private storage.

No data sent to any server

The app transmits no data, neither to us nor to third parties. There is no account, no cloud, no synchronisation. Your duel opponent is a local bot, computed on your own device: nothing is exchanged with another player or with a server. The French dictionary (85,535 words) is bundled inside the app: no network connection is required to play, and the app opens none.

No ads, no trackers

Anagramme chrono shows no advertising. The app contains no analytics, no measurement tool, no crash reporter, and never reads your device's advertising identifier. No Android permission is requested.

No in-app purchases

The app offers nothing for sale. Hints (reveal a letter, freeze the timer) are earned by playing, not bought. No payment method is ever requested, and the app has access to no payment data whatsoever.

Children

Anagramme chrono is suitable for all audiences, children included. Since no data is collected, no children's data is collected either: the app asks for no real name, no age, no email address, no location, no contacts, and no access to the camera or microphone. It contains no advertising, no messaging, no social features and no user-generated content, and allows no communication between players — the opponent is a program, not a person.

Your rights

As no personal data is collected or held outside your device, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. You retain full control of the local data: it is erased from the app's Android settings, or by uninstalling it.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published at this address, with a new update date. Should a future version of Anagramme chrono collect data, this page would be updated before that version is released.
