Anagramme chrono — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026 · Version 1.2

In short

Anagramme chrono is a word game with two modes, and how your data is handled depends entirely on which one you play. The campaign is offline: nothing leaves your device, no account exists. The online duel publishes a round you have just played so that another player can take it on: this requires an anonymous account and sends a small set of game data, described in detail below. There is no advertising, no tracker and no in-app purchase, and nothing is sold or shared with advertisers.

Two modes, two data regimes

  • Campaign (offline) — level-by-level progress against an opponent computed on your own device. No account, no network connection, no data transmitted. A player who never opens the online duel screen never has an account and never transmits anything.
  • Online duel (optional) — you publish a round you have played, another player receives the same letters and watches your score climb second by second. This mode, and only this mode, creates an anonymous account and sends data.

The online mode is an addition, never a toll gate: the app remains fully playable offline and without an account.

What stays on your device

Most of your data never leaves the phone. It is stored in the app's private storage (shared_preferences, on Android):

  • your nickname, chosen by you ("Nino" by default);
  • your progress: campaign levels and records, remaining hints, league points and tier, winning streak, your duel history, best word found;
  • the letter draws you have already seen, so the same letters are never served to you twice;
  • your settings: sounds, vibration, colour-blind mode, timer effects, language.

The French dictionary (85,535 words) is bundled inside the app: playing the campaign requires no connection at all.

The anonymous account used for online duels

Opening the online duel screen creates an anonymous account through Firebase Authentication (Google). "Anonymous" in the technical sense: it does not sign you in to any existing account. The app asks for no name, no email address, no password and no phone number, and does not connect to your Google account. This account is a random identifier, specific to your installation, used only to know which round is yours and to avoid pitting you against yourself.

The connection is lazy: it happens only when you open that screen. As long as you do not open it, no account is created.

What is transmitted when you play online

When you publish a round, or when you answer another player's round, the app sends exactly this:

  • your anonymous identifier;
  • your nickname, as you chose it in the app;
  • the score of the round;
  • the letters of the draw and the dictionary word they come from, so that your opponent faces exactly the same grid;
  • the timestamped list of the words you found — each word, its points, and the second of the round at which you played it. This is what lets your opponent watch you play live even though you played yesterday. Seconds are counted from the start of the round, never as real clock times;
  • the language of the draw and the date it was sent.

Nothing else. No contact details, no location, no device identifier, no freely written content: the words transmitted are French dictionary words you formed from the letters of the draw. A round you abandon is never published.

Your nickname is visible to players who take on your round. It is a free field: we recommend not putting your real name or any identifying information in it.

Where this data is hosted

Shared rounds are stored in Cloud Firestore (Google), in the anagramme-chrono project, in region eur3 — Europe. That choice is deliberate and irreversible: the data stays in Europe. Google acts here as a hosting provider on our behalf. Traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

Retention and deletion

Let us be precise, because this matters: a published round is final. The database security rules forbid any modification and any deletion from the app. You therefore cannot delete a round you have published from within the game. This is a design decision — a duel cannot be rewritten after the fact — but it has to be stated plainly.

Rounds are kept without a predefined duration, so that they remain available to the players who will take them on. To have your published rounds and the anonymous identifier attached to them deleted, write to the contact address below: deletion is carried out manually, within thirty days. Local data, on the other hand, can be erased at any time from the app's Android settings, or by uninstalling it.

No ads, no trackers

Anagramme chrono shows no advertising. The app contains no advertising SDK, no analytics or measurement tool (no Google Analytics, no Firebase Analytics, nor any equivalent), no crash reporter, and never reads the Android advertising identifier (Advertising ID). No data is sold, rented, or passed on to advertisers or data brokers.

No in-app purchases

The app offers nothing for sale. Hints (reveal a letter, freeze the timer) are earned by playing, not bought. No payment method is ever requested, and the app has access to no payment data whatsoever.

Android permissions

  • Internet (INTERNET) — publishing and receiving online duel rounds.
  • Network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) — knowing whether a connection is available, so the app can honestly say "offline" instead of waiting for nothing.
  • Google services (READ_GSERVICES) — required by the Google libraries used to reach the database.

No other permission is requested: no camera, no microphone, no location, no contacts, no storage, no notifications.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you may request access to the information concerning you, its rectification or its deletion, or object to its processing. The processing tied to online duels rests on performing the service you ask for: it starts only once you open that mode and publish a round. One address covers all such requests, given below. Since the identifier is anonymous, please state in your message the nickname you used and the approximate date of the rounds concerned — otherwise we cannot find them.

Children

Anagramme chrono is declared on Google Play for an audience of 9 and over, children included, and therefore falls under the Google Play Families policy. The game asks for no real name, no age, no email address, no phone number, no location and no contacts, and accesses neither the camera nor the microphone.

What is collected from a child is exactly what is collected from an adult — described above — and only if they open the online duel: anonymous identifier, nickname, score, the letters of the draw, and the timestamped list of words found. That data serves only to make the duel work. It is never used to profile, target or contact anyone. There is no advertising — hence no personalised advertising — no advertising identifier is read, no audience measurement, no in-app purchase, and nothing is sold or passed to any commercial third party.

No free exchange between players. There is no messaging, no chat, no comments, no public profile, no photos, and no way to contact another player. The words exchanged are French dictionary words formed from the letters of the draw.

The nickname is the only free field, and it is filtered. It is capped at 14 characters; emoji, symbols, control characters and invisible characters are rejected; and it is checked against a list of offensive terms — the same list used to purge the game's dictionary — on a normalised form, so that workarounds using punctuation, accents or full-width letters fail too. The rejection appears while typing, and the field states, before you confirm, that your opponent will see this name. We advise parents to check that it does not contain the child's real first name.

How to keep a child entirely offline. The campaign, the quick duel and the daily challenge run with no network and no account. As long as the “Online duel” screen is never opened, no account is created and nothing is transmitted: simply do not open it.

Parents and legal guardians may request access to the data attached to their child, its correction or its deletion, by writing to the contact address given below; deletion is carried out within thirty days. Please state the nickname used and the approximate date of the rounds concerned: as the identifier is anonymous, that is the only way to find them. We do not knowingly collect any data from a child beyond what is described here; if you believe a child has sent us more, write to us and we will delete it.
