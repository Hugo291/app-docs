Privacy Policy

MiniGeste — Last updated: May 24, 2026

Introduction

This privacy policy describes how MiniGeste ("the App") handles your information. MiniGeste is a personal budget management application designed to work entirely offline on your device.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. The App does not collect, transmit, or share any information whatsoever. All data you enter into the App stays exclusively on your device.

Specifically, the App does not:

  • Collect personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Collect usage or analytics data
  • Track your location
  • Access your contacts, photos, or other device data
  • Use cookies or similar tracking technologies
  • Contain any advertising or advertising identifiers
  • Make any network requests

Data Storage

All your budget data (income, expenses, envelopes, settings) is stored locally on your device using local storage (Hive database). This data never leaves your device.

If you uninstall the App, all locally stored data will be permanently deleted.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party services, including but not limited to:

  • Analytics services (Google Analytics, Firebase, etc.)
  • Advertising networks
  • Crash reporting tools
  • Cloud storage or backup services
  • Social media integrations

Internet Permission

The App does not require an internet connection and functions entirely offline. No data is transmitted over the network at any time.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. Since the App collects no data from any user, there is no risk of inadvertent collection of children's data.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
