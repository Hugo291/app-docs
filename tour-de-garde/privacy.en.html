Tour de Garde — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026 · Version 1.1 · com.hfc.wave · Français

This policy applies to the Android app Tour de Garde (com.hfc.wave), published on Google Play by PIXELMORE (Hugo Ferreira).

No account, no data collected

PIXELMORE does not collect any personal data through Tour de Garde. The app asks for no account, no sign-up, no email address and no phone number. It does not identify you, and we receive nothing from your device: there is no game server.

Your progress stays on your device

Your won levels, stars, Écus, Forge upgrades, creatures discovered in the Bestiary and your settings (music, sound effects, vibration, speed, language) are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone, in a local database (Hive). They are sent to no one. If this save file became unreadable, the app keeps a copy of it in that same private space before starting over with a fresh game.

One exception, which depends on you: if Android Backup is enabled on your phone, Android may include this data, like any app's data, in your Google account backup, which we cannot access.

Offline

The game works fully offline: playing, your progress and your save never go through the Internet. Only the optional purchase described below goes through Google Play, and so through the network.

One optional purchase, through Google Play

The whole campaign can be played without paying anything. The Premium in-app purchase (a one-time purchase, no subscription) unlocks ×5 speed during the assault; it is only offered when you tap the ×5 button or the Premium row in Settings.

  • Payment is handled entirely by Google Play, under the Google Play Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Your payment details are entered in Google Play: neither the app nor PIXELMORE can access them.
  • To unlock Premium, the app receives from Google Play, on your phone, the confirmation of the purchase (product ID, purchase state and purchase token), sends it back to Google Play to acknowledge it, then records “Premium bought” in the local save. It sends nothing to PIXELMORE: there is still no game server.
  • “Restore my purchases”, as well as a silent check when the game starts, asks Google Play for the purchases linked to your Google account, to bring Premium back after a reinstall or on another phone.
  • As with any sale on Google Play, Google provides PIXELMORE, in the Play Console, with the order information needed to manage sales (for example the order number, product, date, price and country). PIXELMORE uses it only for accounting, refunds and support.

No ads, no trackers

This version contains no advertising, no advertising ID, no audience-measurement tool (no Google Analytics, no Firebase), no crash reporting and no third-party tracking library. No data is sold, rented or passed to data brokers.

Distribution through Google Play: for users who have turned on usage and diagnostics sharing on their phone, Google may provide PIXELMORE, in the Play Console, with aggregated statistics (installs, crashes). These statistics come from Google Play, not from the app.

Android permissions

The app uses no sensitive permission: no camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage or notifications. It declares:

  • Google Play billing (com.android.vending.BILLING) — for the optional Premium purchase.
  • Internet and network state access (android.permission.INTERNET, android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) — added by Google Play's billing library for its exchanges with Google; the game itself does not use them.
  • Internal permission (com.hfc.wave.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION) — added by AndroidX; it keeps the app's internal messages private.

The game's sounds are played from the app's own audio files. If you turn on the Vibration option (off by default), the game vibrates your phone through the system's haptic feedback; this stays local.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or using Android Settings → Apps → Tour de Garde → Storage → Clear data, permanently deletes your progress from the phone. If Android Backup is enabled, a copy may remain in your Google account backup, managed in your phone's backup settings. A Premium purchase stays linked to your Google Play account, which keeps it: “Restore my purchases” finds it again.

Your rights

Apart from the order information Google Play sends for a Premium purchase, we hold no data that can identify you. Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure and objection; there is nothing to rectify or erase on our side, but you can write to us at the address below. You may also lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

Children

Tour de Garde is not directed at children under 13: the audience declared on Google Play starts at 13. We collect no data, and therefore none from a child. If you are a parent or guardian with a question, write to the address below.

Future versions

If a later version added other optional in-app purchases or optional rewarded ads, this policy would be updated before that version is released, to describe precisely the services involved, the data processed and your choices (including consent). As long as this page does not say so, the game contains none.
