Tour de Garde — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026 · Version 2.0 (app 1.0.4 and later) · com.hfc.wave · Français

This policy applies to the Android app Tour de Garde (com.hfc.wave), published on Google Play by PIXELMORE (Hugo Ferreira). It describes exactly what the app does with your data.

What the app does not do

No game account is required: no sign-up, no email address, no phone number. There is no game server: nothing you do in Tour de Garde is sent to PIXELMORE. The app does not access your contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera, and contains no audience-measurement tool (no Google Analytics, no Firebase), no crash reporting and no tracking library. No data is sold, rented or passed to data brokers.

Four Google services are involved, though, each one only when you choose to use it: purchases (Google Play), the rewarded video “Free Écus” (Google AdMob), Google Play Games and, if you open it, the game's Google Play page (sharing, rating). They are described below.

Your progress stays on your device

Your levels won, stars, Écus, Forge upgrades, troops and Sky Strikes in reserve, the creatures discovered in the Bestiary, today's count of “Free Écus” and your settings (music, sound effects, vibration, speed, language) are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone, in a local database (Hive). It also keeps the “Premium purchased” flag and the Google Play tokens of the latest Écu purchases already credited (so the same purchase is never credited twice). This data is sent to no one, except to Google if Google Play Games is signed in (see below). If this save file ever became unreadable, the app keeps a copy of it in the same private storage before starting a fresh game.

If Android backup is turned on on your phone, Android may include this data, like that of any app, in your Google account backup, which we cannot access.

Offline

The game works fully offline: playing, progress and the local save need no Internet. Only purchases, the rewarded video, Google Play Games, sharing a victory and opening the Google Play page use the network.

Optional purchases, through Google Play

The whole campaign can be played without paying anything. The app offers, with no subscription:

  • Premium, a one-time purchase: unlocks ×5 speed during the assault and guarantees that no ad will ever be offered to you (“Free Écus” are then collected with one tap, without a video).
  • Purses of Écus (consumable purchases, in the Shop): add Écus to your balance.
  • Payment is handled entirely by Google Play, under the Google Play Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Your payment details are entered in Google Play: neither the app nor PIXELMORE can access them.
  • To deliver a purchase, the app receives its confirmation from Google Play, on your phone (product ID, purchase state and purchase token), sends it back to Google Play to acknowledge or consume it, then records the result in the local save. It sends nothing to PIXELMORE.
  • “Restore my purchases”, as well as a silent check when the game starts, asks Google Play for the purchases linked to your Google account, to bring Premium back after a reinstall or on another phone, and to deliver a purse of Écus left pending.
  • As with any sale on Google Play, Google provides PIXELMORE, in the Play Console, with the order information needed to manage sales (for example the order number, product, date, price and country). PIXELMORE uses it only for accounting, refunds and support.

Advertising: an optional video for Écus

In the Shop, the “Free Écus” card offers to watch a rewarded video ad to earn 100 Écus, up to 3 times a day. It is the app's only advertising: no banner, no forced ad, no ad between levels. If you never tap “Watch”, no ad is ever shown. A Premium player never sees a video.

These videos are provided by Google AdMob. Google's advertising module (Google Mobile Ads) is neither started nor contacted before your first tap on “Watch”. To select and measure ads and to fight fraud, Google then processes your device's advertising ID (and app set ID), your IP address (from which it infers an approximate location), information about your device and the app, your interactions with the ad (viewed, tapped, closed) and diagnostic information (performance of the advertising module). This collection is Google's: the app does not access or store this data, and PIXELMORE only receives totals from AdMob (number of impressions, revenue). Google uses it under its advertising policies and its Privacy Policy.

You stay in control of the advertising ID: Android Settings → Security & privacy → Privacy → Ads, where you can reset or delete it (Google help). Ad personalization can also be managed in your Google account: myadcenter.google.com.

Your consent (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland)

If you play from one of these countries, Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) is shown at your first “Watch”, before any ad, and no ad is requested from AdMob before you answer. You can accept, refuse, or choose purpose by purpose. Refusing does not stop you from playing: the whole game stays available, and ads may then be non-personalized, or not shown.

You can change this choice at any time in Settings → “Ad privacy” (a line shown where the law requires it), or by resetting your phone's advertising ID.

Google Play Games, optional

The game offers the achievements, a leaderboard and the cloud save of Google Play Games. None of it is needed to play. These services are only active when a Play Games profile is signed in to the game: either because Play Games signs you in to its games automatically (a setting of your Play Games profile), or because you tap “Achievements” or “Leaderboard” in Settings and accept Google's prompt.

  • What is then sent to Google, through the Google Play Games service: the achievements you unlock, your total of stars (the “Stars” leaderboard) and a copy of your progress (levels, stars, Écus, Forge, reserves, Bestiary), stored in the saved games of your Google account. Neither the Premium purchase, nor purchase tokens, nor your settings are part of it.
  • At launch, if the copy in your Google account is further ahead than the one on the phone (reinstall, new phone), it replaces it.
  • On the leaderboard, your Play Games name and your score are visible to other players, according to the privacy settings of your Play Games profile.
  • This data is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. The app reads neither your name nor your email address and sends nothing to PIXELMORE. Google gives PIXELMORE, in the Play Console, aggregated statistics about achievements and the leaderboard.

Sharing a victory and rating, when you choose to

After a victory over a boss, a button lets you share a message (the boss defeated, the chapter and a link to the game's Google Play page). The app then opens X's post page in your browser, or Android's share menu: you choose whether to send it, and to whom. The game contains no social network SDK and sends nothing by itself. The Écus given for the first share are added to the local save.

After the victory over Archmage Noctis (level 5-6), a window invites you, with nothing promised in return, to rate the game on Google Play; the “Rate Tour de Garde” line in Settings does the same. Tapping the button opens the game's page in Google Play, where the rating is given; the app sees neither the rating nor the review.

These links carry a source tag (utm_source) that only tells Google Play that a visit comes from a share or from the game, without identifying you.

Google Play statistics

For users who have turned on usage and diagnostics sharing on their phone, Google may provide PIXELMORE, in the Play Console, with aggregated statistics (installs, crashes). These statistics come from Google Play, not from the app.

Android permissions

The app uses no sensitive permission: no camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage or notifications. It declares:

  • Google Play billing (com.android.vending.BILLING) — for the purchases described above.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) and Android ad services (android.permission.ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS) — added by Google's advertising module, for the rewarded video; they are only used after your first “Watch”.
  • Internet and network state (android.permission.INTERNET, android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) — for Google's libraries (billing, ads, Play Games) to talk to Google; the game itself does not need them.
  • Background tasks (android.permission.WAKE_LOCK, android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE) — added by Google and AndroidX libraries to finish their network exchanges; the game does not use them.
  • Internal permission (com.hfc.wave.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION) — added automatically by AndroidX; it keeps the app's internal messages private.

The game's sounds are played from the app's own audio files. If you turn on the Vibration option (off by default), the game vibrates the phone through the system's haptic feedback; this stays local.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or using Android Settings → Apps → Tour de Garde → Storage → Clear data, permanently deletes your progress from the phone. If Android backup is on, a copy may remain in your Google account backup; it is managed in your phone's backup settings. A Premium purchase stays linked to your Google Play account, which keeps it: “Restore my purchases” brings it back. Écus bought and spent are not restored.

If Google Play Games was signed in, your achievements, score and the copy of your progress are kept by Google: you delete them in the Google Play Games app (Settings → Delete Play Games account & data → Tour de Garde), or from your Google account. For data processed by Google AdMob, use the advertising ID settings described above and your Google account.

Your rights

Apart from the order information Google Play sends for a purchase, we hold no data that identifies you; Google Play Games and Google AdMob data are held by Google, which is responsible for it and with which you can exercise your rights (access, erasure, objection, withdrawal of consent). Under the GDPR you also have, towards us, the rights of access, rectification, erasure and objection; there is nothing to rectify or erase on our side, but you can write to us at the address below. You can lodge a complaint with the CNIL or the data protection authority of your country.

Children

Tour de Garde is not directed at children under 13: the audience declared on Google Play starts at 13. The game asks for no personal data and has no communication between players. If you are a parent or guardian and see an inappropriate ad, or have a question, write to us at the address below.
