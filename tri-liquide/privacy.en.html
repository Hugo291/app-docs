Tri liquide — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026 · Version 1.0

Tri liquide is an offline puzzle game. This page describes exactly what the app does with your data. It is short because there is not much to say.

What the app itself collects: nothing

No account is required. The app does not access your contacts, location, photos, microphone, camera or address book. It contains no analytics tools and sends no data to any server of ours — there is none.

Two Google services are involved, however, and they do collect data: billing, and the rewarded video. Both are described below.

The rewarded video

The game offers one hint per hour in exchange for watching a short video. It never starts on its own: you have to tap "+1 hint". There are no banners, no ads between levels, no interruptions.

These videos are served by Google AdMob. To select them, AdMob may read your device's advertising ID along with technical information (model, operating system, language, approximate IP address). We have no access to any of it and receive none of this data.

You stay in control of that identifier: Android Settings → Security & privacy → Privacy → Ads, where you can reset or delete it.

Processing by AdMob is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and by How Google uses information.

What is stored on your phone, and never leaves it

The app keeps three things in the private storage area the system reserves for it:

What is keptWhy
Stars earned per levelTo restore your progress
Remaining hint countSo you do not lose them when closing the game
Your settingsBackground colour, sounds, music, vibration, colour-blind mode, left-handed mode

This data never leaves the device. It is sent to no server, neither ours — there is none — nor a third party's. It disappears entirely when you uninstall the app.

Purchases

In-app purchases — hint packs only — are handled by Google Play, never by us. We see neither your payment method, nor your name, nor your address: Google only tells us that a purchase was validated, so the app can credit what was paid for.

Google's processing of that data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is not specifically directed at children. It has no player-to-player communication and asks for no personal data, but it does offer purchases and video ads: it therefore targets a general audience.

Your rights

We hold no data about you: there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. For data held by Google, contact Google, or reset your advertising ID from the Android settings. To erase all local data, simply uninstall the app, or use Android Settings → Apps → Tri liquide → Storage → Clear data.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that collects data — analytics, an online leaderboard — this page will be updated before that version goes live, and the Play Store listing will say so.
