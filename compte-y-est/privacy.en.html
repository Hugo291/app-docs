Le Compte y est — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026 · Version 1.1

What stays on your phone

Le Compte y est requires no account and never identifies you. Your settings (timer duration, tile draw composition, theme) and your progress (statistics, best times, day streak) are stored on your device only. Puzzles and solutions are computed locally: the game is fully playable offline.

Only two features leave the phone, and both are described below: the video ad for a hint and the daily challenge ranking. We never ask for an email, a name, a phone number, or any other identifying information.

Advertising, only when you ask for a hint

No ad is shown while you play: no banner, no interstitial between games, no screen at the end of a game. The only advertising in the app is a video you request yourself by tapping "Hint" and confirming. If you never ask for a hint, no ad is ever loaded.

These videos are served by Google AdMob. To deliver them and count views, AdMob receives your Android advertising ID, your IP address (hence an approximate, city or region level location), your interactions with the video and technical information about the device. This data is used to display the ad, measure its delivery and detect fraud. Details: Google Privacy Policy.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a Google consent form appears before the first video. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

The daily challenge ranking

The daily draw is the same for everyone. On your first attempt of the day, the app sends your result so the end screen can place you among the other players ("Better than X% of players").

What is sent: the distance to the target, the points, the time taken, the draw date, and a random installation ID (32 characters drawn at random the first time the app opens). That ID is not tied to you, to your Google account or to your phone, and it changes if you reinstall the app. No nickname, no email, no address.

These results are stored in our own Google Firestore database (project compte-y-est, servers located in the European Union), acting as a host on our behalf. They are never sold to anyone. The app never downloads another player's result: it only retrieves counts (number of players, number of exact hits, number of results worse than yours). Offline, the daily draw remains playable and your streak is kept: only the ranking disappears.

No audience measurement, no analytics: Google Analytics is disabled on this project, and the app embeds no behavioural tracking SDK.

One-time purchase via Google Play

The app offers a one-time purchase (no subscription) to unlock the full version. This purchase is handled entirely by Google Play (Google Play Billing): we never see, receive or store any payment data — no card number, no identity, no billing address. The only effect inside the app is a "purchased" flag stored locally on your device, which also lets you recover your purchase after reinstalling. Google's processing of the payment is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

No sensitive permissions requested

The app requests no sensitive Android permission: no location, no contacts, no photos, no microphone, no camera, no shared storage. The only permission included are the Google Play billing one (com.android.vending.BILLING), required for the one-time purchase described above, Internet access (hint video and daily ranking) and the advertising ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID), added by the AdMob kit.

Local data storage

Your settings and progress are stored locally by the app (built with Flutter) using Hive, a local database kept in a private file the system reserves for the app and which no other app can access. Concretely, it holds:

  • Your settings — timer duration, tile draw composition, light/dark theme.
  • Your statistics — games played, exact-answer rate, best times, 30-day curve, distribution by distance to the target.
  • The daily challenge — your attempt of the day and your streak of consecutive days.
  • The purchase flag — a simple "purchased" marker once the full version has been unlocked.

This data never leaves your device. Only the values described above (your daily draw result and the random installation ID) are sent, and only for the ranking.

Deleting your data

You stay in full control of your data. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all the data it has stored on your device. On Android, you can also use Settings → Apps → Le Compte y est → Storage → Clear data. Your one-time purchase, however, remains attached to your Google Play account and can be restored after reinstalling.

The result sent for the daily ranking cannot be withdrawn on request: it is tied to no person, so we cannot tell which one is yours. Reinstalling the app creates a new installation ID with no link to the old one.

Children

Le Compte y est is aimed at an adult audience and is not specifically directed at children. The game has no player-to-player communication and asks for no personal data, whatever the age of the person playing. The only possible ad is the hint video, which the player triggers themselves.

Your rights

We hold no data that identifies you: ranking results are anonymous and cannot be linked to a person, so there is nothing to access or correct on our side. Game data stays on your device and is entirely yours to manage. For advertising, write to us or change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy; for payment-related data, please contact Google Play, the only party that processes it.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app undergoes major changes. If a future version were to add anything that collects data, this page would be updated before that version goes live. The last update date is shown at the top of this page.
