Le Livre de Recettes de Famille — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026 · Version 1.0

No data collection

Le Livre de Recettes de Famille does not collect any personal data. The app runs entirely offline (offline-first). All your recipes, photos, settings and backups are stored exclusively on your device, in the app's local Hive database.

No transmission to our servers

The app performs no network communication with our servers or any third-party servers. There is no backend, no server and no cloud. Your data never leaves your phone.

No account required

You do not need to create an account to use the app. We do not ask for an email, name, phone number, or any other identifying information.

No advertising, no tracking

The app contains no advertising, no advertising tracking SDKs, no Advertising ID, no analytics (Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.), and no user behavior measurement tools.

Android permissions requested

Every permission below is used 100% locally on your device. The content involved is never sent over the internet.

  • Camera — to scan a recipe-sharing QR code and to take a photo of a dish. The image is processed locally and is never transmitted.
  • Photos / gallery — to pick a photo to illustrate a recipe. The selected photo stays on your device.
  • Files / storage — to import and export recipes and backups (.ilivrecette files) that you choose yourself. These files stay local.
  • Printing / PDF generation — to generate the recipe book as a PDF locally, directly on your device.

Deleting your data

You can delete your recipes and data at any time from within the app. Uninstalling the app also deletes all the data it has stored on your device.

Exporting and backing up your data

The app offers export and backup functions for your recipes (.ilivrecette files) as well as PDF book generation. These operations are entirely local: the files produced are saved wherever you decide on your device and are not transmitted to any server.

Children

The app is not directed at children under the age of 13. Since no data is collected from any user, there is no risk of inadvertent collection of children's data.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app undergoes major changes. The last update date is shown at the top of this page.
