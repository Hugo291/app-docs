Sudoku Classique — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026 · Version 1.0

No data collection

Sudoku Classique does not collect any personal data. The app is a Sudoku game that requires no account and never identifies the user. Your settings (light/dark theme, text size, language, visual assists, sound and vibration) and your progress (game in progress, statistics, best times, achievements, daily puzzle) are stored exclusively on your device.

No personal data sent to our servers

The app does not transmit any personal data to our servers or to third parties. No account, no cloud, no synchronization of your information. Sudoku puzzles are generated entirely on your device: the game works 100% offline and needs no Internet access at all.

No account required

You do not need to create an account to use Sudoku Classique. We do not ask for an email, name, phone number, or any other identifying information.

No advertising, no tracking

The app contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no advertising tracking SDKs, no advertising identifier, no analytics (Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.), and no user behavior measurement tools.

No permissions requested

The app requests no Android permission: no network access, no location, no contacts, no photos, no microphone, no camera, no shared storage.

Local data storage

Your settings and progress are stored locally by the app (built with Flutter for Android and iOS) using shared_preferences, that is, in a private file the system reserves for the app and which no other app can access. Concretely, three things are kept there:

  • Your settings — theme, text size, language, highlighting, error flagging, sound, vibration.
  • The game in progress — so you find it exactly as you left it when reopening the app.
  • Your statistics — games started and completed, best times per difficulty, streaks, achievements earned, daily puzzle calendar.

This data never leaves your device.

Deleting your data

You stay in full control of your data. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all the data it has stored on your device. On Android, you can also use Settings → Apps → Sudoku Classique → Storage → Clear data.

Children

Sudoku Classique is aimed at an adult audience and is not specifically directed at children. The game has no player-to-player communication, no advertising and no purchases, and asks for no personal data, whatever the age of the person playing.

Your rights

We hold no data about you: there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. The only data that exists is what stays on your device, and it is entirely yours to manage.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app undergoes major changes. If a future version were to add anything that collects data, this page would be updated before that version goes live. The last update date is shown at the top of this page.
