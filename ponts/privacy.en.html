Ponts — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026 · Version 2.1 (app 2.1.0 and later)

Ponts is a puzzle game (the Japanese hashi puzzle) played offline, with no account. This page describes exactly what the app does with your data.

What the app does not do

No account is required. We run no server: nothing you do in the game is sent to us. The app does not access your contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera. It contains no analytics (no Google Analytics, no Firebase, nothing of the kind).

Three Google services are involved: advertising (Google AdMob), billing (Google Play) and, if you wish, achievements (Google Play Games, optional). They are described below. At launch, the app also reads a small public file that says whether a free period is under way (see "Free period").

Advertising

The app shows at most one full-screen ad (interstitial), when you leave the "Grid solved" screen. Never during a game, never at launch, no banners. Ads are deliberately rare:

  • no ads during the first 3 days, counted from installation (or from the first launch of version 2.1 if you already had the app); during that time the app already contacts AdMob (consent message, preparing an ad) but shows none;
  • after that, at most one ad every 3 solved grids, and 2 per day at most.

The "The full archipelago" purchase removes ads permanently. Ads are also suspended during a period when all 150 grids are free (see "Free period").

These ads are served by Google AdMob. To select and measure them and to fight fraud, AdMob collects your device's advertising ID, your IP address (from which it infers an approximate location), your interactions with ads (viewed, tapped, closed) and diagnostic information (performance, ad module crashes). Google uses this data for its own advertising services. We do not see it in detail: AdMob only shows us totals (impressions, revenue).

You stay in control of the advertising ID: Android Settings → Security & privacy → Privacy → Ads, where you can reset or delete it. Once you purchase, and during a free period, the app does not contact AdMob.

Your consent (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland)

If you play from one of these regions, no ad is requested from AdMob until you have answered Google's consent message, shown when the app opens (it is not shown after the purchase or during a free period, since AdMob is then not contacted). You can accept, decline ("Do not consent"), or choose purpose by purpose ("Manage options"). Declining does not stop you from playing: ads may then be non-personalised, or not shown.

If you accept, AdMob may use the advertising ID and store information on your device to personalise and measure ads. You can change your mind at any time from the app's privacy options, or by resetting your phone's advertising ID.

Free period

From time to time, all 150 grids may be offered to everyone for a limited time. To find out, the app reads a small public file at launch, https://hugo291.github.io/app-docs/ponts/promo.json, hosted by GitHub Pages. It only says whether a free period is under way. No personal data and no identifier is sent; as with any web page, GitHub sees the IP address of the request. Without a connection, the app keeps the last response received.

During a free period, the app does not contact AdMob (see "Advertising"). Grids you solve or start during the period remain playable afterwards.

Google Play Games achievements (optional)

Ponts offers 15 Google Play Games achievements. This is optional: playing requires no account. A switch in Settings, on by default, controls this feature.

  • Switch on: at launch, the app checks, without displaying anything, whether you are signed in to Google Play Games; you can sign in from Settings. If you are signed in, Google Play Games receives your Play Games player ID and the achievements you unlock. This data is processed by Google under its own privacy policy. The app receives no other data about your account from Google.
  • Switch off: the app sends no achievements to Google Play Games and does not offer to sign in. The Google Play Games module built into the game still checks on its own, at launch, whether a Play Games profile is signed in on the phone (Google's behaviour, which the app cannot turn off).

Purchases through Google Play

The app offers, with no subscription:

  • A one-time purchase "The full archipelago": unlocks all 150 grids, removes ads and raises the free hints to 5 a day instead of 3.
  • Hint packs (consumable purchases): add hints to your balance. Three hints a day are free, no purchase needed.

These purchases are handled entirely by Google Play (Google Play Billing): we never see, receive or store any payment data — no card number, no identity, no billing address. The price shown is the one Google Play gives you. The app only keeps a "purchased" marker and your hint balance, stored locally. "Restore my purchases" (and a quiet check at startup) recovers the one-time purchase after a reinstall. The hint balance is not backed up outside the device. Google's handling of payments is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Android permissions

No sensitive permission. The app uses Internet access (needed for ads, the free-period file and Google Play Games achievements) and the Google Play billing permission (com.android.vending.BILLING), plus the technical permissions of Google's ad module (network state, advertising ID). The game itself runs without a connection.

Local data storage

Your settings and progress are saved locally using Hive, a local database kept in a private file reserved to the app by the system. It holds:

  • Your settings — theme, island size, timer display, greying of finished islands, Google Play Games achievements switch.
  • Your progress — solved grids per pack, daily grid, best times, game in progress.
  • The hint economy — free hints used today, purchased balance, IDs of purchases already credited.
  • The purchase marker, the first-launch date, the count of grids solved since the last ad, the number of ads shown today and the last response of the free-period file.

This data is sent nowhere. (Google Play Games achievements, if you have them enabled and are signed in, are sent to Google as described above.) The daily grid is computed on your phone from the date: no download.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes the data it stored on your device (progress and hint balance included). On Android you can also use Settings → Apps → Ponts → Storage → Clear data. Your purchases stay tied to your Google Play account. Achievements recorded by Google Play Games stay with Google, which is responsible for them. For data processed by Google (advertising, payment, achievements), use your Google account tools: myaccount.google.com.

Children

Ponts is aimed at an adult audience and does not specifically target children. The game has no player-to-player communication and asks for no personal data. Ads are served by AdMob, which must not be used to target children: if you are a parent and see an unsuitable ad, please write to us.

Your rights

We hold no data about you: there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Data processed by Google AdMob, Google Play and Google Play Games is processed by Google, which is responsible for it (the IP address seen when the free-period file is read is seen by GitHub); you can exercise your rights (access, erasure, objection, withdrawal of consent) with Google. Data kept on your device is yours to manage freely.

Changes to this policy
