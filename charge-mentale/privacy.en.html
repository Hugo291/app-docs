Charge Mentale — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026 · Version 1.0

No data collection

Charge Mentale does not collect any personal data. The app runs entirely offline (offline-first). All your tasks, settings and history are stored exclusively on your device, in the app's local Hive database.

No transmission to our servers

The app performs no network communication with our servers or any third-party servers. There is no server, no cloud, no synchronization. Your data never leaves your phone.

No account required

You do not need to create an account to use Charge Mentale. We do not ask for an email, name, phone number, or any other identifying information.

No advertising, no tracking

The app contains no advertising, no advertising tracking SDKs, no advertising identifier, no analytics (Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.), and no user behavior measurement tools.

Android permissions requested

The app requests the following permission:

  • Notifications — only if you enable reminders in settings. Notifications are scheduled locally by the Android OS and require no network connection; no data is sent.

Deleting your data

You stay in full control of your data. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all the data it has stored on your device.

Intended audience

Charge Mentale is intended for an adult audience (18 and over) and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app undergoes major changes. The last update date is shown at the top of this page.
