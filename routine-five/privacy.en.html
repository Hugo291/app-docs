Routine Five — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026 · Version 1.0

No data collection

Routine Five does not collect any personal data. The app runs entirely offline (offline-first). All your habits, settings and history are stored exclusively on your device, in the app's local Hive database.

No transmission to our servers

The app performs no network communication with our servers or any third-party servers. Your data never leaves your phone.

No account required

You do not need to create an account to use Routine Five. We do not ask for an email, name, phone number, or any other identifying information.

No advertising, no tracking

The app contains no advertising, no advertising tracking SDKs, no analytics (Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.), and no user behavior measurement tools.

Android permissions requested

The app requests the following permission:

  • Notifications — only if you enable daily reminders in settings. Notifications are scheduled locally by the Android OS and require no network connection.

Deleting your data

You can delete all your data at any time using the "Erase everything" button in the app settings. Uninstalling the app also deletes all the data it has stored.

Exporting your data

The app offers an "Export my data" function that copies all your habits and history as JSON to your device's clipboard. This export stays local and is not transmitted to any server.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app undergoes major changes. The last update date is shown at the top of this page.
